Design Choice

I kept the website simple as to keep the content the website displays the focal point. I went with Courier New font as it is proffesional rather than a silly font. I also went with #E3DCDC which gave the background a light grayish pink that was easy to contrast against the text and compliments the reddish color (#FF0145) used in the header and in the graphs. Everything was centered to make it easy to read on the screen as well as rounded corners for a softer layout.
